# ☕ A Cup of Care

Welcome to **A Cup of Care** — a cozy little café chatbot that’s always here when you need a warm word or virtual hug.  
Feeling sad, tired, anxious, or just “meh”? Pull up a chair. We’ve got you. 🌸
